Stock Watcher — RAG Chatbot for Market Data
Sep 2026
Solo developer · Personal project

Dec 2025 – Present
Solo developer · Prototype
  • Independently built the full technical stack — auth, database, storefront, and checkout — for a blind-box bracelet e-commerce concept, from the Postgres schema through the checkout flow customers actually use.
